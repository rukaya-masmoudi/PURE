# Identity as data

Identity in PURE is not a “profile page”.

It is the way my behaviour and decisions appear when they are turned into data.

---

## What Identity is today

Today, Identity is visible through:

- Studies Layer
  - What I choose to study
  - How long I study
  - How difficult it feels
  - How much energy I have

- Life Layer
  - Where I show up in the community
  - Which roles I take (attendee, speaker, community-collaborator)
  - Which events are important enough to be documented

- Calendar
  - When learning and events coexist
  - When I only study
  - When I only go to events

- Career & Roles:
  - Where I studied
  - Where I have worked
  - Where I contribute as a volunteer over time

Identity is not a static description.
It is a pattern across these layers.

---

## Why data-first Identity

PURE starts from data before AI because:

- Data makes growth measurable
- Data makes narratives auditable
- Data makes AI explainable later

This also means:

- No inflated roles
- No invented events
- No artificial scores

The system is as honest as the data you can query.

---

## What Identity will not become

Identity in PURE will not be:

- An endless list of achievements
- A gallery of AI experiments
- A static CV copy

It will remain:

- Structured
- Measurable
- Connected to real behaviour

Future layers (Intelligence, App, Governance) will build on this,
not replace it.
